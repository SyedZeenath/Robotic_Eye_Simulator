Photo Angle Measurer

The zoom helps you see the pixel, but a mouse click is still limited to the screen's resolution, not the photo's, so it can't reliably land on the exact same image pixel twice. To actually get pixel-repeatable points: every canvas below now shows the live cursor position in real image pixels, click a point roughly where you want it, then use the arrow keys to nudge the last point 1px at a time (hold Shift for 5px) while watching the X/Y readout, or just type the exact X,Y pixel values into the boxes and hit "Add point at typed pixel" to place it with no mouse imprecision at all. Note down the X/Y numbers for a point you like, and you can type those exact same numbers back in next time.

Mode 1: Torsion (in-plane rotation angle)

Use this for torsion only. Click three points in order:

  1. Pivot (red) - center of the pupil/iris (torsion rotates around this point, it does not move)
  2. Fixed reference (blue) - an identifiable mark on the STATIC housing (paint dot, screw, seam) that does NOT move with the eye. Defines your 0deg direction.
  3. Moving marker (green) - a specific identifiable spot in the iris texture pattern, placed near the EDGE of the iris, well away from the pivot. The farther from the pivot, the more precise the reading.

Mode 2: Vertical / Horizontal (displacement angle)

Vertical and horizontal eye movement is NOT an in-plane rotation as seen from a straight-on camera - it shows up as the pupil sliding up/down or left/right in the frame. This mode measures that displacement and converts it to degrees using the eye's own radius, so it needs a fixed camera position (tripod) between the neutral shot and every test shot - if the camera moves, the numbers are meaningless.

Step A: set the neutral position (eye at 0,0,0)

Load the neutral photo. First click 3 to 8 points spread around the IRIS edge (the blue/dark ring, not the white sclera edge) — more points, spread further around the visible arc, gives a better fit. Click "Finish iris" when done, it'll fit a circle through your points and show it. Then do the same for the PUPIL edge (the black circle in the middle) and click "Finish pupil".
